To someone special

Dear Lumi,

I don't know exactly where to start, because there are so many things I could say. But maybe the best place is simply this: I'm really glad that God made our paths cross once again. This was supposed to be my way of confessing my admiration for you from the moment we started talking, yet you had other plans in mind. I've said this once but I'll say it again, I have never stopped thanking God for giving me this opportunity to be this close to you. I know that I've already gave you a quick glimpse of our story from my perspective, but allow me to paint you the bigger picture.

I'll be honest, I was scared. The beginning quarter of this year was one of the most challenging and heartbreaking moments of my life. I became a shell of myself, someone that I barely knew, but I'm glad that my faith in God was the very light that guided me to where I am today. One banter, one joke, one tease from the others was enough to rekindle a feeling that I felt 7 years ago, the feeling of hopelessly falling for someone that I knew was too far to reach. But despite that, I still believed in hope.

You can call me weird for having a crush on someone that I didn't even as much as so see most of the time, but it was real. The idea of you and me was so bleak in my mind, yet merely thinking about you would easily send me in a daze. The idea was hopeless, but I had hope myself.

The reason why I insisted on visiting HRPC the very first time that we met was because that shrine saw me at my highest and at my lowest. Through my brightest days and my darkest of nights, I knelt before that shrine. I prayed that God would finally make me meet the person I could truly love with all my heart, and even though it seemed hopeless, I prayed that it was you.

I never expected all of this to happen, heck I didn't even expect you to reply back to me from the time that I replied to your story—but you did. You've honestly left me on read many times before that already, which is why I didn't expect anything after that. But our conversation would eventually grow longer and longer as every single day passes by, and I couldn't help but be excited for simple "goodmorninggg" messages every time I woke up. I thought you were only being nice or friendly, like the person I knew you were, which is why I held onto my feelings for as long as I could. You opening up about some other guy and telling me that you didn't like him really didn't reaassure my chances of confessing to you, if anything, it only made me fear it more. And yet, you had other plans yourself. (wala eh inunahan moko hay)

“It's the feeling I get when I look in your eyes”

It's so unfair that whenever I keep staring at your eyes, it pulls me in deeper into loving you even more. Whenever I'm around you, whenever I'm blessed with your presence around, whenever you're talking about school, passion, or anything in general, I can't help but admire you even more. From the way that you genuinely love helping others, taking care of patients and other families you didn't even know before your duties, or your passion in doing something good for humanity—such as outreaches, it's the same exact characteristic that made me fall for you in the first place. I know I've already said the very things that made me admire you from my sudden confession weeks ago, but I hope you know that I still mean every single one of them.

“It's the time that we spend doing nothing at all”

From our first "date" up until now, I still get butterflies when I'm around you. I can't help but always smile whenever I walk the streets of Angeles beside you. If you ask me if I ever get tired of waiting, I don't. I was able to wait months for you, what more for a couple of hours just to go home with you?

“It's the pact that we make to be safe and secure”

There are moments where I really would love to just hold your hand while we walk at night, but I know I still don't have that privilege yet, but know that I'm only waiting for your lead. I never want to make you feel uncomfortable, which is why I still get shy at doing acts of service towards you. But I promise you that I'll do the very best that I can to make you feel safe whenever you're around me. I want you to feel secure and protected whenever you're around my arms, the person that takes the weight off your shoulders and allows you to feel peace and warmth within their presence.

“It's when you hold my hand and assure me”

Even though these words can never quite hold everything that I want to tell you, I hope these ones come close enough. I hope they make you smile, even just a little, just like what you do for me. Thank you for assuring me at my weakest state, for encouraging me on days that I feel defeated, and for being by my side whenever I feel alone and disappointed. Words and actions that may seem little to you, but mean the whole world to me.

“So if you ask me what love is, I'd shrug and I'd smile”

The Rob before thought that he'd lost the meaning of love, but in the end, you became the very reason that he started learning to love again. Thank you for being the light that I asked for, the peace in the storm that felt difficult to conquer, and for being my answered prayer.

“Rest your head and lay down for a while”

For as long as I'm here, you will always have someone to lean on to. Someone to rest your head after a tiring day, someone to rant to during your busy days, someone you can yap anything your carefree and random mind may think of, someone that will love you for who you are unconditionally. I promised you that I'll never become a burden, and I plan on keeping that promise. I promise that the way I treat you, the way I look after you, and the way I take care of you now will always be the same for every single passing day. After all, the very person who takes care of others needs someone to take care of them too, right?

“There's so much to say, and so much to do”

I could keep writing this love letter even longer, but it's very essence will always stay the same. I hope that in this not-so-short message, you understand how much of an amazing person you are. I hope that you understand how much you mean to me, how much am I willing to wait, how much am I willing do to just to protect and take care of you. Thank you for being you.

“I'd love to figure love out with you”

I know you are still new to these things, and I promise that I'll be gentle and patient with you. I told you that I wouldn't rush you, and I mean that wholeheartedly. Please don't think that I'm rushing you to figure it all out, or that I'm expecting you to have all the answers right now. Take your time. Let yourself feel things at your own pace, and let whatever this is between us unfold naturally. I don't want you to feel like you have to learn what love is just because I'm here telling you how I feel. I want you to discover it for yourself, in your own time, in your own way. But if there's one thing I hope for, amidst all the things you have yet to figure out, it's that when the time comes for you to understand what love means to you, I hope I'm right there beside you. And if love really is something we're meant to figure out along the way, I'd love to figure love out with you.

Can I court you? - Robbi
